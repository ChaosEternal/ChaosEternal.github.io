Daily Financial, Tech & AI News Digest -- 2026-11-13

Market Overview

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's CEO on Thursday, and US stocks slipped as the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.1%. The S&P 500 fell 0.32% to 7,595.16, the Dow fell 0.20% to 52,380.61 and the Nasdaq fell 0.48% to 25,342.89. Oil rose while bitcoin and gold fell. October producer prices are due on Friday. (Yahoo Finance)

Financial News

Nvidia closed down 5% after Jensen Huang resigned. The stock had opened down 14% and recovered most of the loss during the session. AMD and Broadcom gained on the day. (Yahoo Finance, Eurasia Business News)

The 10-year Treasury yield rose 11 basis points to 5.107%. The 2-year yield jumped 14 basis points to 4.867%. Traders priced in more Federal Reserve rate hikes. (CNBC)

Oil rose after President Trump said the US does not need a deal with Iran. December WTI settled up 2.8% at $88.97, and Brent traded near $95. (MLQ, Yahoo Finance)

Jobless claims rose to 200,000, and existing home sales hit a 16-year low. Claims for the week ended 7 November were up 9,000, against 195,000 expected. Continuing claims fell 20,000 to 1.697 million. October existing home sales were the lowest in more than 16 years. (Reuters via WTAQ)

Bitcoin fell 4.3% to about $71,410, and gold fell 2.8% to $3,887. Both fell as yields rose. (Yahoo Finance)

Intuitive Machines rose 18% and Paysafe fell 30% on earnings. Intuitive Machines reported record quarterly revenue of about $269 million and a $1.9 billion backlog. Paysafe missed on both earnings and revenue. (Kalkine, StockAnalysis)

Tech News

Jensen Huang resigned from Nvidia with immediate effect. He left his roles as president, CEO and board member. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and Harvey C. Jones becomes the board's first chair. The resignation came days after Nvidia opened an investigation into reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. On Hacker News the AP story drew 2,216 points. (Nvidia, NPR, HN)

Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast model for coding and agent work. Google says it completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash. It is free to all users, including in Google Search. API pricing is $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. (Google, HN)

Outlook, Teams and Microsoft 365 went down across several regions. Microsoft said a large number of servers failed automated health checks and dropped out. That shifted load onto the remaining infrastructure. The incident ID is EX1550543. (TechCrunch, HN)

United Airlines banned personal AI agents from booking flights. Its contract of carriage now forbids agents to make, modify or service a ticket. United cites credential-sharing risks and federal rules that require a human to complete check-in. Delta and American still allow some AI tools to book. (CBS News, TechCrunch)

A critical remote code execution flaw hit Apache HTTP Server's HTTP/2 module. CVE-2026-119618 is a race condition in mod_http2 that affects versions 2.4.55 to 2.4.70. Anthropic and Google found it independently. Version 2.4.71 fixes it, and no in-the-wild exploitation is known. (Wiz, CSA)

Rust 1.100 was released. It drew 300 points on Hacker News. (Release notes, HN)

Nintendo took down Switch emulators and mod-chip firmware in a large GitHub DMCA sweep. (IGN, HN)

AI Papers & Discussions

Continual-ARC compares memory-based and weight-based learners on one lifelong stream (arXiv:2611.14505). It is built from all three ARC-AGI editions, whose tasks need no domain knowledge. Tasks return after dormant gaps with fresh instances. The learner can pay for demonstrations, answer, or act inside its harness. It matters because it scores agent harnesses as a continual-learning mechanism next to test-time weight updates.

Contrastive Discovery lets research agents find which explanation the evidence supports (arXiv:2611.14514). A pointwise agent tests one hypothesis and learns only that it is unlikely. This paper scores each experiment against a full set of competing explanations instead. On the same evidence, it resolved 73% of rejected candidates to a specific alternative. With the same experiment budget it also resolved 67% more questions.

ARE-bench tests research agents on ongoing projects (arXiv:2611.14364). Most benchmarks hand an agent a clean, self-contained task. ARE-bench gives it one step of a real project, with earlier decisions, stale files and failed attempts in the workspace. It checks whether the agent respects that prior state.
