Financial News

Anthropic's IPO book is six times oversubscribed. Anthropic is offering 825 million shares at $70 to $78, about $64 billion, and bankers are weighing a higher price. The deal prices on Wednesday and trades from Thursday. (Unite.AI, CNBC)

The New York Times reports that Howard Lutnick pushed for weaker AI chip export rules. His version would cover seven countries, including Singapore and Malaysia, and apply only to future shipments. Cantor Fitzgerald, his former firm, has clients among data centers those rules would touch. The Times found no evidence that Cantor influenced his decisions, and Commerce denies any link. (NYT via Inquirer, HN)

A DRAM price-fixing suit against Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron survived a motion to dismiss. The plaintiffs say the three conspired to cut conventional DRAM supply by moving capacity to AI memory. A Northern District of California judge ruled on Friday that the claim is plausible under the Sherman Act. (LitNews, HN)

UK chip startup Fractile raised $720 million at a $7 billion valuation. Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed and Redpoint led the round, and the UK government's Sovereign AI fund joined. Fractile stacks DRAM on its inference chips and plans to ship its first one next year. (TechCrunch)

Japan publishes third-quarter GDP today at 08:50 Tokyo time. Economists expect 0.3% annualised growth, down from 1.4%. They expect private consumption to fall 0.2%, its first drop in seven quarters. (IndexBox)

US retail sales and industrial production come out on Tuesday, and the Fed's October minutes on Wednesday. They are the week's main US data releases. Jobless claims follow on Thursday. (CNBC)

AI Papers & Discussions

MAPE evolves whole agent-memory programs instead of hand-designing them (arXiv:2611.17068). On LoCoMo and LongMemEval it gains up to 12 points of accuracy at 35% of the serving cost. Two patterns explain most of the gain. Raw conversations kept as evidence preserve details that extracted memories drop, and retrieved evidence works better arranged by the query's intent. Adding the second pattern to five existing memory systems improved all five. (arXiv)

ACE-RL trains an agent to write the playbook its later attempts inherit (arXiv:2611.16409). One model acts, reflects and curates the playbook. Each candidate playbook is scored by how well later rollouts do with it. It beats baselines on held-out agentic benchmarks, which argues for training the handoff, not only the actions. (arXiv)

LLMs can write neural network weights by hand for unseen games (arXiv:2611.16981). Given simulator source, the best writer built a 57k-parameter network that solved 56% of held-out levels. The same writers' Python programs solved 97-100%. The written networks look like programs: 93% of weights are zero. PPO fine-tuning destroyed most of their skill. (arXiv)

PREWARN scores failure monitors on whether they fire before a failure starts (arXiv:2611.16803). On repetitive degeneration, response-level AUROC saturates, and a rule that reads only response length scores almost perfectly. Probes that AUROC ranks as equal differ widely in how early they warn. (arXiv)

r/MachineLearning: "[Meta FAIR + NYU] Unbundling Multimodal Vision Encoding: training 200+ MLLMs to find encoder pretraining is optional but vision-only parameters are not [R]". Title only; the feed carries no post body. (Reddit)

Reasoning-vs-Knowledge Watch

Pretraining data decides when a model stops guessing from frequency and starts reasoning (arXiv:2611.16882). On entity comparison, small models pick the more frequent entity first, then the one in a fixed position, and only later the right answer. A uniform entity distribution weakens the frequency phase and speeds up learning. The same three phases appear in OLMo-2 32B. (arXiv)

Self-distillation gains on maths come from the teacher's thinking mode, not the worked solution it sees (arXiv:2611.16064). Teachers given only the problem often match teachers given the solution. The authors call this "reasoning arbitrage". Accuracy gains therefore do not show that the privileged knowledge transferred. (arXiv)

Adding teacher reasoning traces to LoRA fine-tuning hurt in-domain results on financial tasks (arXiv:2611.17072). Label-only LoRA on Qwen3-8B did better in-domain and kept general skills near the frozen model. Training on traces left in-domain metrics behind and changed retention benchmarks little. (arXiv)
